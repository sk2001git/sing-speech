What are the benefits of the new framework under the HCSA as compared to the previous framework under the PHMCA? Are there any overseas countries that utilise a similar licensing framework?
The PHMCA was premises-based whereas the HCSA adopts a service-based licensing structure. This is similar to the healthcare systems in the United Kingdom (UK) and Malaysia.
This allows licensees the flexibility to provide a range of services and take on new licences in a modular fashion.
