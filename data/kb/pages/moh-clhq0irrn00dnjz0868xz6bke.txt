I am a patient ≥ 60 years old. What should I do if I become unwell?

Medically vulnerable persons (i.e. elderly persons aged 65 years and above and those with chronic medical conditions) are at greater risk of severe disease or complications when infected with COVID-19. If you develop ARI symptoms, or have severe, prolonged or worsening ARI symptoms, you should consult your doctor for medical management.
