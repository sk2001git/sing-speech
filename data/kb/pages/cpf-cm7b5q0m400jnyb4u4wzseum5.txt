When will my eligibility for the Matched MediSave Scheme be assessed and do I need to apply?
Members’ eligibility for the Matched MediSave Scheme (MMSS) will be automatically assessed annually. You do not need to apply for the scheme.
MMSS is implemented in January 2026 for five years. You will be notified at the beginning of each year if you are eligible for the scheme for the year via email or post. Eligibility information will be available around the 2nd or 3rd week of January of each year and notifications to eligible members will be sent out around end January.
You can also check your eligibility for the MMSS via the Healthcare dashboard.
