I am well but I had tested positive for COVID-19. What should I do?

While there are no legal requirements for COVID-19-positive individuals to isolate, you are strongly encouraged to exercise personal and social responsibility to reduce transmission risks. In particular, persons who test positive for COVID-19 should:
· Stay away from work, school, or public places until their symptoms have fully resolved. If there is a need to go out while symptomatic, they should minimise social interactions, wear a mask, avoid crowded places and meeting medically vulnerable persons such as seniors or immunocompromised patients.
· Practise good hygiene, such as frequent handwashing and covering their mouth and nose when coughing or sneezing.
