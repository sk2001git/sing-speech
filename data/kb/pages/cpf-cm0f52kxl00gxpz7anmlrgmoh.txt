How do I check my voluntary top-up limit to my MediSave Account?
When you make a MediSave top-up online, you will be shown the amount of MediSave top-up you can receive before you reach the top-up limit.
You can follow these steps to check how much you can top up to your MediSave Account:
Go to the Top up MediSave Account form
Log in with your Singpass
Select “I wish to make a top-up to Myself” under “Recipient details”
Your top-up limit will then be displayed.
If you are making a top-up to your loved ones, please check with your recipient directly on the amount he can receive.
