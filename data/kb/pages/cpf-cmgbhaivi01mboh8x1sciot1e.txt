How much CPF payouts can I get every month?
Your monthly payout amount depends on the amount of savings in your Retirement Account (RA) and your age at the point you start your payouts. Under CPF LIFE, you can receive up to $3,440 per month for life starting from age 65. For each year that you defer, your payouts will increase by up to 7%. This means that you can receive up to $4,580 if you choose to start your payout at age 70. You can use the Retirement Payout Planner to estimate how much you need every month to support the retirement lifestyle you want and project your CPF payout based on your CPF balances.
