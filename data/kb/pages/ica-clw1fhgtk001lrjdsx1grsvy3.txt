How do I know that the death has been registered if I do not need to register the death? How do I obtain a death certificate?
After the doctor certifies the death online, you can visit the My Legacy website to download the digital death certificate.
To access and download the digital death certificate, you will require the following information from the certifying doctor or hospital:
• Death certificate number (or Confirmation of Death document number)
• Deceased’s Identification Document number
• Deceased’s date of death
You may refer to ICA's website for more information.
