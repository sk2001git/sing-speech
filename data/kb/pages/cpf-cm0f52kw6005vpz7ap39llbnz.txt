How do I request an instalment plan for my MediSave contributions?
You can request an instalment plan to make your MediSave contributions in monthly instalments via the Manage your MediSave payable form.
If you have an existing instalment plan and wish to lower your existing monthly instalment amount, you can submit a request via the same form.
Please note that your request is subject to the Board’s approval. You may be required to provide additional information or supporting documents, if necessary.
