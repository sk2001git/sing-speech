Under the Healthier SG Chronic Tier, can I receive subsidies for chronic medications that are not found on the Healthier SG Medication List?
Yes, CHAS, Merdeka Generation (MG) and Pioneer Generation (PG) cardholders can still receive dollar-based subsidies between $28 and $135 per visit, subjected to their eligible annual cap (based on their CHAS/MG/PG card status).
Healthier SG benefits and subsidies are available/accessible at your enrolled Healthier SG clinic.
