How can I apply? (CHAS)
You can apply for CHAS via the online application by using this link.
Alternatively, you can also pick up a CHAS application form at any Community Centre or Club (CC), Community Development Council (CDC), Public Hospital or Polyclinic or download the application form by using this link . Please send the completed and signed application form to Privy Box No. 920231, Singapore 929292.
If anyone in your household is not a Singapore Citizen, please also include copies of their foreign identification documents (e.g. work permit/long term visit pass).
If your application is successful, all eligible members of your household will receive an individual CHAS card in their name. The card indicates the subsidy tier you qualify for (CHAS Blue or Orange or Green) and is valid for two years. Simply present the card at a CHAS GP or dental clinic when seeking treatment. PG and MG cardholders can use their PG or MG card at any CHAS clinic to obtain CHAS subsidies. You will receive an itemised bill for your treatment.
If this is your first visit to a CHAS GP, please also present your NRIC upon registration (or birth certificate for cardholders under 15 years old).
By using your CHAS or Pioneer Generation or Merdeka Generation card at a CHAS clinic, you consent to allow your doctor/dentist to disclose your personal information to the Ministry of Health (including its authorised agents).
