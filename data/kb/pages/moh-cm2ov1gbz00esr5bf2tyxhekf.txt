I would like to appeal for the Merdeka Generation Package. Can I do so, and how?
Citizens aged 60 years and above in 2019 (i.e. born on or earlier than 1959) who became Singapore citizens after 31 December 1996, but have good claims to be counted among the MG, can appeal for the MGP.
Similar to the Pioneer Generation Package, the Appeals Panel will not consider appeals from those who miss out on the age criterion (i.e. born after 31 December 1959 in this case).
You can send your appeals to contactus@merdeka.gov.sg or call 1800 2222 888. You can also mail to Robinson Road P.O. Box 3060 Singapore 905060.
