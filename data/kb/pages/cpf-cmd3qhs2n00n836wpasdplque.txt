If your date of birth is before 1 July 1960, you can view the past FRS (PDF, 49KB).
You can set aside your FRS with a mixture of CPF savings in your Retirement Account (RA) and CPF savings used for your property.
If you are 55 and above, you can choose to set aside more in your RA via top ups, up to the current Enhanced Retirement Sum, for higher monthly payouts.
