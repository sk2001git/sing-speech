What consumables are covered under SMF?
You can use SMF subsidies for the following types of consumables (also known as home healthcare items):
Adult diapers
Catheters and catheter set
Milk feeds
Nasal tubing
Nasogastric tubes and pH indication test strips
Thickeners
Wound dressings
