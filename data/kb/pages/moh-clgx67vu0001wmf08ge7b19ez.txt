If I do not have enough in my MediSave, and I am not able to pay the remaining co-payment, are there other ways to receive the vaccination?
At CHAS GP clinics, these nationally recommended vaccinations are fully subsidised for those who hold the Public Assistance card.
Patients who go to the polyclinics for their vaccination(s) and have financial difficulties in paying for their bills can approach the social worker at polyclinics to enquire for assistance.
