Can I use MediSave to pay for the removal of my wisdom tooth?
You can use your MediSave savings to pay for the removal of your wisdom tooth incurred at a participating private dental clinic under the MediSave Scheme, provided that it is a surgical procedure. The day surgery limits are:
1) Day surgeryUp to a maximum of $830 per day for daily hospital charges, including investigations, medicines and doctor's attendance fees.
2) OperationA fixed limit for operations ranging from $240 to $5,290 depending on the type of operations performed.
