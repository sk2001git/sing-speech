What do I need?
For nomination, you will need the following:
• Your Singpass
• Your nominees’ NRIC/FIN and (optional) email address.
• Two witnesses, Full Name, NRIC/FIN, email address and Singapore-registered mobile phone number.
Note:
If your nominees or witness is a foreigner, you will need to furnish their mailing address as well.
Your witnesses cannot be your nominees.
You can visit any ServiceSG Centres to seek assistance to make an online Nomination. Click here for the list of ServiceSG Centres locations and their operating hours
