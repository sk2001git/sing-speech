How do I view my CPF Yearly Statement of Account?

You can view, print and/or download your CPF Yearly Statement of Account for the past 10 years via Singpass login at my cpf digital services. As we do not provide CPF statements beyond the past 10 years, please download your statements in a timely manner to ensure that you always have a copy for your own records. To find your downloaded documents on mobile devices, refer to this FAQ.
For non-Singapore Citizens and non-Permanent Residents, please refer to our FAQ for more information.
This information is sourced from CPF.
