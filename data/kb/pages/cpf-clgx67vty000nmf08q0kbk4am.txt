Can MediSave be used for the co-payment after subsidy for vaccinations?
Those eligible for subsidised vaccination may also use their MediSave to cover the out-of-pocket expense at CHAS GP clinics and polyclinics.
Please check with your preferred clinic if the clinic is able to process the remaining co-payment via MediSave. If they do, please check with them on your eligibility to pay via MediSave.
