How will I receive my payouts?
You will receive your payouts through your PayNow-NRIC linked account, GIRO or GovCash. You are encouraged to link your NRIC to PayNow to receive your payouts earlier.
For step-by-step guidance on setting up your PayNow-NRIC account, please visit: CPFB | What is PayNow and how do I link my NRIC to PayNow?
