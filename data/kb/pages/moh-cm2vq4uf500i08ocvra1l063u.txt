I am applying for subsidy from another healthcare scheme, but was directed to approach MediShield Life. What can I do to have my means-test result reassessed so that I can be eligible for higher subsidy?
To reassess your household and/or means information for MediShield Life Premium Subsidies and/or other selected healthcare schemes, please submit your request via the MediShield Life e-Service (https://medishieldlife.moh.gov.sg/). We will contact you if we require further clarification on your request. Alternatively, you may wish to call the hotline at 1800-222-3399.
