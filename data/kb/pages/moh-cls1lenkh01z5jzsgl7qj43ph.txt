Can I apply for ComCare assistance if I have refunded my SIRS payout?
Low-income households that are unable to afford their basic living expenses can approach MSF’s Social Service Offices (SSOs) to apply for ComCare.
If you need financial assistance, you may email Ask_SSO@msf.gov.sg, call the ComCare hotline at 1800-222-0000, or approach the nearest Social Service Office.
