My mobile number/email address is outdated. How can I update my records in Singpass?

To update your mobile number/email address with Singpass, please complete the following steps:
Login to the Singpass website
Select ‘Menu’ > ‘Update profile’ (under the ‘My Account’ tab) and follow the instructions on the website to complete the updating of your mobile number/email address
Please ensure that you have access to your mobile phone/email as a One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your updated mobile number/email address for verification.
If you require further assistance, please contact the Singpass Helpdesk directly. The operating hours are from 8am to 8pm (Mondays to Fridays) and from 8am to 2pm (Saturdays) excluding Sundays and public holidays.
This information is sourced from CPF.
