Where can I access subsidised referrals to public specialist outpatient clinics (SOCs)?
Eligible individuals can access subsidised referrals to public specialist outpatient clinics (SOCs) regardless of their Healthier SG enrolment status, as follows:
Singapore Citizens: You can get subsidised referrals at polyclinics. If you hold a CHAS, Merdeka Generation (MG), or Pioneer Generation (PG) card, you can also get subsidised referrals at CHAS clinics.
Permanent Residents: You can get subsidised referrals at polyclinics.
Enrolment in Healthier SG is not required for subsidised referrals.
