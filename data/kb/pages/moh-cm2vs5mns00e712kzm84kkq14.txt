How can I find out if I am covered under MediShield Life or an Integrated Shield Plan?
You are covered under MediShield Life if you are a Singaporean or Permanent Resident.
If you are covered under an Integrated Shield Plan (IP), your premium payer will be notified by your private insurer with details about your premiums and subsidies when your IP is up for annual renewal. You will receive applicable subsidies for the MediShield Life portion of your IP, if you meet the eligibility criteria.
You can log in to visit the Healthcare dashboard (cpf.gov.sg/healthcare) 'Coverage and premium details' to check the coverage and premium information for yourself and the dependant(s) you are paying premiums for.
