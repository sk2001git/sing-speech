Why is my clinic no longer a Healthier SG Clinic?
This could be due to various reasons, such as, your clinic has ceased operations, changed ownership, or is no longer participating in the Healthier SG scheme. This means that you will not be able to enjoy Healthier SG benefits at this clinic anymore.
You are encouraged to continue your Healthier SG journey by enrolling to a new preferred Healthier SG clinic via HealthHub. If you need assistance with enrolment, you may approach a nearby Healthier SG Enrolment Station, a participating clinic at https://gowhere.gov.sg/healthiersg. For further queries you may also contact the MOH Hotline at 6325 9220.
