What document will I receive from the hospital or doctor who registered the death?
After a death or stillbirth has been certified, you'll receive 1 document from the hospital or doctor.
This can be any of the following:
National Electronic Health Record (NEHR) printout
Electronic Registration of Births and Deaths (eRBD) printout
Confirmation of Death (COD) or Confirmation of Stillbirth (COS)
If you received a Certificate of Cause of Death (CCOD), please contact ICA at Go.gov.sg or 6391 6100 for assistance.
View sample documents (PDF)
If the death was certified outside of a hospital, you may receive a photo of the document instead of a printout.
No matter which document you receive, you should download the digital death or stillbirth certificate. You'll need it to arrange the cremation or burial, manage estate settlement, and more.
However, you can also use the NEHR printout, COD, or COS to:
apply for a burial or cremation permit with NEA
book an HDB wake space with the town council
