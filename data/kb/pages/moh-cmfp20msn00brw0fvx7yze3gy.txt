What is the maximum amount of premium discounts that could be redeemed?
Under the regular conversion rate of 150 Healthpoints to $1, the maximum value of cumulative Healthpoints that an individual can earn in a calendar year is worth $270.
As the MediShield Life premium discounts have a higher conversion rate of 150 Healthpoints to $2, the maximum value of premium discounts that could be redeemed per calendar year is $540. Please refer to this webpage for more details.
